THE GOBLIN WARREN

Who sees this page
RegionThornwood
DangerModerate

A sinkhole in the Thornwood opens into a warren of low tunnels. The goblins have lined the main hall with stolen bedrolls and a long table taken from the Hollins farm.

Hidden

Grukk Twotooth keeps the Hollins reliquary under the bedrolls in the east chamber. A DC 13 Wisdom (Perception) check spots the loose stones.

The warren answers to Grukk Twotooth, who rarely leaves the east chamber.

Revealed to Lyra EmberwiseSESSION 4

Scratched into the west wall in Goblin: "Grukk takes the shiny things east."
