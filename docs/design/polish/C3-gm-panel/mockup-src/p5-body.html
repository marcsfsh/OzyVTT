The GM's panel, and what waits on the GM

Approved after one round. What waits on the GM sits at the top of the Encounter tab, or in its own tab: the GM switches between the two from the list's head, and Settings' Device section keeps the choice. Each item is a card. The panel's tabs are words with counts, and a count is what is new since the tab was last opened, so it drops when the tab opens. A bell with its count says something waits on the GM, everywhere it waits. In the frames, press the panel's tabs (and the phone's Switch) to watch their counts drop, and press Own tab or Over the turn in the list's head. The switches still show the options that were compared; the chosen ones are marked. On a phone, the bar at the top switches pages and options, and the laptop view opens close up: swipe it sideways.
