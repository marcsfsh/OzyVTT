Familiar sheets

Three layouts modeled on character sheets players already know: D&D Beyond, Foundry, and the printed sheet that Roll20 follows. Each has a single row of tabs and keeps hit points and AC in view, in the Horizon style with as little decoration as it can carry. The controls work: a bonus rolls when you tap it, and Heal and Damage change the hit points. Slot boxes tick off as you spend them.
