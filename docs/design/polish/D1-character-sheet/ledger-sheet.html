Character sheet: Ledger

The approved layout, drawn to the values in the implementation plan. A vitals band runs across the top on every screen. On a laptop, abilities and their saves run down the first column, skills fill the second and the tabbed box the third. On a phone the band and the tabs stay pinned and Main holds the abilities and skills. The controls work: tap a number to roll it, and Damage and Heal change the hit points. The toast stands in for the app's own roll feedback.
