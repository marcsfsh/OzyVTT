The Atlas

Approved after one round. The Atlas puts the maps at the side as a tree, with the layers under it, the map in the middle, and the pins at the right. A pin chosen in that list or on the map opens in the right column as its page, with All pins at its head and The party's position and Players see it as switches; a right-click on a pin opens its own menu, with the same two actions. Editing a pin keeps that column, with Look, Links, Encounters and Who sees it as tabs. The story's Downtime tab puts the characters at the side. Every part is one already settled (Carried in). The switches still show the options that were compared; the chosen ones are marked. Scroll past the switches and a compact bar keeps the page and its options in reach.
