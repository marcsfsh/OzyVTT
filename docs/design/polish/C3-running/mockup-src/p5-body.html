Running the encounter

Approved after one round. Starting an encounter is one form; a creature's tools open under its row in the order, which widens while open; the encounter's options are a menu at the end of the panel's tabs; adding monsters is the Compendium's three panes in a dialog; and a roll inside a waiting card is the button itself. Every part is one already settled (Carried in). The switches still show the options that were compared; the chosen ones are marked. On a phone, the bar at the top switches pages and options, and the laptop view opens close up: swipe it sideways.
