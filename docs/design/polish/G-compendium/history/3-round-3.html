Browsing and open records

One question this round: how the filter panel and records opened beside the list work as one place to browse. Home and the studio stay as approved. Each option is drawn in four states: browsing with three records already open, an SRD monster open, your copy open, and ticking a filter while a record is open. Every option draws the record's Card, Sharing and History as a small switch in its head, so there are never three rows of tabs, and none of them folds the filters to a sideways label. In the frames, press the open records, the record's Card, Sharing and History, and Filters on a phone. On a phone, the bar at the top switches states and options, and the laptop view opens close up: swipe it sideways.
