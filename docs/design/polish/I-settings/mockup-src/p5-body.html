Settings

Approved after one round. Settings is a side list of its sections, each with a line of what it holds now, and the search at the top of the list; on a phone the list is the first page and a section opens with Back. Each setting is a row with its name, its line and its control at the right. Encounters groups its rows by when they matter, and Access keeps its rows and the credentials' table. The switches still show the options that were compared; the chosen ones are marked. On a phone, the bar at the top switches pages and options, and the laptop view opens close up: swipe it sideways.
