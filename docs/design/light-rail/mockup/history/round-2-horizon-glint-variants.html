OzyVTT Horizon Glint

OzyVTT motion study, round two

Horizon glint

Four takes on the horizon glint, each a little more visible than the first round, with option 1 as the refined original. Every option has a page version for moving between sections and an in-page version for tabs. The Compendium now has its own tabs as a second place to try the in-page version.

Your device asks for reduced motion. The demo still plays these when you click, so you can judge them; in the app they would be instant for you.

1

Glint, refined

The first glint, tightened up. Tapping a section lights its underline, and that light ignites a spark on the horizon. Two comets with tapered tails run out to the edges while a cone of light, coloured by that stretch of the horizon, falls onto the page.

Page330 MS

A flash under the tapped section starts it. The comets slow as they near the edges and the whole line lifts for a moment, while the view fades in over 180 ms.

In-page330 MS

The underline stretches to the new tab. Its leading edge arrives first and the trailing edge catches up, lit in the horizon colours on the way, and a small spark pops where it lands.

Since round one
Tapered comets replace the soft blobs, and the underline now starts the spark. The wash is a narrower cone.
To build it
Everything but the wash lives in the top bar or the tab strip, so transforms are safe there. The stretch is two transitions on the underline's left and right edges.
2

Light rail

The light travels from where you were to where you're going. The old section's underline drains into a comet that runs along the horizon, then fills the new underline from the side it arrived on.

Page460 MS

The comet takes 230 ms whatever the distance, then bursts on arrival and drops a short wash onto the page. The view starts fading in as the comet leaves.

In-page380 MS

The same trip runs along the bottom edge of the tab strip, from the old tab to the new one. The panel fades in as the light lands.

Since round one
It borrows Tape shuttle's sense of direction without moving the page.
To build it
The comet and burst sit in the top bar and the tab strip, and the page only fades, so the pane rule holds. The comet's start and end come from the two buttons' positions.
3

Horizon bloom

The whole horizon flares at once. The line swells to twice its height, and a broad glow in its colours blooms down over the top of the page, then draws back up into the line.

Page340 MS

There's no single point of origin, so it reads the same wherever you tap. The view fades in under the glow.

In-page300 MS

The divider under the tabs becomes a small horizon for a moment. It lights outward from the tab you picked, then fades back to a plain line.

Since round one
This is the loudest of the four. It gives up the spark's precision for a softer, wider light that feels closer to a sunrise.
To build it
The glow is one layer above the page that scales and fades, with nothing fixed inside it, so transforming it is safe. The tab version is one more line in the Tabs primitive.
4

Chrome catch

The light lands on the lettering. A quick spark runs along the horizon, and the new page's title catches it as a slice of the horizon's colours sweeps once across the letters.

Page490 MS

The tapped section's label catches the same sweep. The view has faded in long before the sweep finishes, so nothing waits on it.

In-page440 MS

The new tab's label catches the sweep, and so does the panel's title where it has one, as in the Compendium.

Since round one
It moves the light off the page's ground and onto its words, so there's no wash.
To build it
The sweep is a moving gradient clipped to the text, added for one pass and removed after. A heading with the heading shadow needs the shadow off during the sweep, or the shadow covers it.

Timings on the cards are how long the light lasts, from tap to the last fade. That runs past the app's 120 to 200 ms motion rule in every option, but the content can be used at once and has fully faded in within 300 ms, so the rule would need a line that allows longer decorative light. Under reduced motion, each would be an instant swap.
