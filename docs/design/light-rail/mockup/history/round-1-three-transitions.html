Page transitions

Three candidates to replace the scanline wipe. Each one has a page version for moving between sections, like Codex to Compendium, and a quieter in-page version for tabs inside a page, like the character sheet's Spells and Gear tabs.

Your device asks for reduced motion. The demo still plays these when you click, so you can judge them; in the app they would be instant for you.

Chroma settle

The colour bleed of a worn tape. The new view arrives with its cyan and pink channels a few pixels apart, and they slide back into register as it fades up.

Page200 MS

Text and panel edges start 6 px out of register and settle while the view fades in.

In-page150 MS

Only the text splits, by 3 px, and the panel fades up from 30%.

Why it fits
The wordmark already wears this exact split, so the motion borrows a mark the app has. Dawn gets the softer split its lettering uses.
To build it
Only shadows and opacity animate, so the pane's no-transform rule holds. Headings that set their own shadow need the keyframe as well.
2

Horizon glint

The neon line under the top bar does the work. A spark runs out along it from the section you picked, and a faint wash of its colour falls on the page as the page fades up.

Page200 MS

The spark reaches both edges of the bar. The wash starts at 20% under the tapped section and is gone by the time the view has faded in.

In-page200 MS

The tab underline slides to the new tab, lit in the horizon colours while it moves and cyan once it lands. The panel fades in behind it.

Why it fits
The horizon line already marks the top of the app, and the sliding underline moves the way the sun frame does between turns.
To build it
The spark sits in the top bar, outside the pane, so it can use transforms. The wash is one opacity layer, and the underline is one new element in the Tabs primitive.
3

Tape shuttle

Fast-forward and rewind. The new view slides in from the side you're heading toward. On a page change, faint tracking lines streak across with it.

Page200 MS

The view slides 18 px and fades in while three 1 px lines in the horizon colours cross the pane.

In-page160 MS

The panel slides 10 px and fades in, with no lines.

Why it fits
Moving right through the sections fast-forwards and moving left rewinds, so the motion also tells you which way you went.
To build it
This is the one that moves the view, which today's pane rule forbids because a transform traps fixed overlays. It needs the View Transitions API, which animates a snapshot of the page, or a transform on an inner wrapper with no fixed overlays inside.

Every timing stays inside the app's 120 to 200 ms motion rule, and each would be an instant swap under reduced motion. Current wipe is in the picker for comparison: the scanline band on page changes, with sheet tabs swapping instantly as they do today.
