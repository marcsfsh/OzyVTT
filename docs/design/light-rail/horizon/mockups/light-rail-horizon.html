    OzyVTT motion system

    Light rail

    One motion language for every change in the app. Light travels along a line that's already on screen, from where something was to where it's going. It drains out of the old place, runs as a comet, bursts on arrival and fills the new place. A short wash falls onto whatever changed. The content itself only fades, so nothing waits on the light.

    The player above has one live stage per group. Pick a group along the top, then a moment along the bottom, and it plays. Play runs it again, and you can also use a stage directly where it has controls. Slow, 6x in the bar stretches every timing so you can study it.

    The Tone switch in the bar compares the approved light (Before) with Horizon, which carries the Cup button's look into every moment. Each lit line is a horizon, cyan to yellow along its own length, with a faint cast of the same colours beside it. A comet takes the horizon's colour where it leaves and where it lands, and a burst takes the colour where it lands. Hit points, damage, healing and targeting keep their own colours, because those colours mean something. Plain display lettering moves to Chakra Petch, and chrome lettering stays Bungee.

    Your device asks for reduced motion. The stages still play when you press, so you can judge them; in the app these would be instant for you.

    The six parts

    Drain
    The old place lets go of its light, shrinking toward the direction of travel.
    Comet
    A bright head with a tapered tail runs along an existing line. Its tail stretches mid-trip and shrinks into the head on arrival.
    Fill
    The new place lights from the side the comet arrived on.
    Burst
    One short flash where the light lands. Dusk gives it a glow; Dawn shows only the flash on the line.
    Wash
    A faint fall of the line's own colour onto what changed, gone in a quarter second.
    Content
    The thing you asked for. It starts fading in at 60 ms and is complete by 260 ms.

    Rules

    What the Motion section of the design doc would say if Light rail is adopted. These replace the current 120 to 200 ms rule and the tab fade.

    1. Light travels from the old place to the new one.

      It drains out of the old place, runs as one comet, bursts and fills where it lands. A wash is optional.

    2. Light rides lines that are already there.

      The horizon, tab strips, the top edge of a surface, a bar or a rail of pips. Nothing gets a new line just to carry light.

    3. Content never waits.

      It starts fading in within 60 ms and is complete within 300 ms. The light around it ends within 500 ms, except for sequences that light several things in order, like a long rest or the start of an encounter, which may run to 700 ms.

    4. One pass per change.

      Light never loops or pulses. A moment plays again only when the thing happens again.

    5. The page itself only fades.

      Light lives in bars, strips and overlays, so the pane keeps its no-transform rule and fixed overlays stay put.

    6. Waiting is still.

      Something waiting on a person shows WAIT on a grey, unlit line and doesn't move. When it resolves, light runs along that line.

    7. Lists that reorder slide.

      When items change places, each slides from where it was in 240 ms, and light runs along the rail between the old place and the new one.

    8. Each 80s element keeps its job.

      Status uses its own lines, and chrome lettering only catches light when it names who's acting.

    9. Dawn has no glow.

      The burst is a flash on the line and the wash is half as strong.

    10. Reduced motion skips the light.

      Content swaps instantly. Reduced transparency drops the wash.
